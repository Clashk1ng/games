How it plays

One finger. One hungry monster.

Hold and slide to steer. The bars fall and sway, the cubes drift, and the rows keep getting faster.

  • Hold, slide, chomp

    Your monster runs toward your finger. Slip through the gap in every falling row and chomp the cubes for 2 points each.

  • Grab the snail

    When the rows get fast, the snail slows everything down for a breather. Then the speed builds again.

  • Nine candy colours

    Every run drops you into one of 9 colours. Your monster, the bars, the walls and the cubes all match.

A pure game.

No pop-ups between runs, nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for. Your Top 10 lives on your device, and every run gets one free Second Chance.
