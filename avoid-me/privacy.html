Avoid Me · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Avoid Me collects no personal data. This page explains exactly what that means. Last updated 1 October 2026.

The short version

  • No personal data collected
  • Scores and progress stay on your device
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • No account or sign-in
  • Works fully offline
  • Safe for children

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Avoid Me app for iPhone and iPad, and the same game wherever else it is offered. "We" means the developer of Avoid Me.

Information we collect

None. Avoid Me does not collect, share or sell any personal information, and it never sends anything about you or your play to us or to anyone else. There is no sign-up, no contact form and no way to send us data from inside the game.

We never see your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or how you play.

What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress, Avoid Me keeps a small save in the app's own storage on your device. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

What is savedWhy
Your best scoreShown on the home screen and the game-over card.
Your settings (music, sound effects, vibration, reduce motion, controls, drag speed)So the game remembers your choices.

If your device backs up apps (for example with iCloud Backup), this save can be included in your own backup, under your own account. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes the save.

Ads, analytics and tracking

Avoid Me shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. It uses no cookies and no advertising identifier, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Accounts and purchases

There are no accounts, logins or in-app purchases. Everything in the game is available from the start.

Internet and permissions

Avoid Me works fully offline. Everything it needs is inside the app, and it makes no network connections while you play. It does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Avoid Me is made to be safe for players of all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live). There are no ads, no chat, no links to websites and no way to contact other players inside the game.

The App Store

Apple handles the download of Avoid Me under its own privacy policy. Apple may give developers anonymous, combined statistics, such as the number of downloads, and crash reports only if you chose to share them with app developers in your device settings. These cannot identify you and are not collected by the game.

Your choices

You control everything the game keeps. Change your settings at any time in the game's Settings, and delete the app to erase its save. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you have a question, write to us.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Avoid Me changes anything described here, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
