How it plays

One rule: avoid the red.

A white dot, a dark arena and everything red trying to catch you. Simple to learn, hard to survive.

  • Every threat warns you

    Bars pulse on the edge they come from, chasers show a chevron, launchers draw their aim. It is hard, never cheap.

  • Lure them into the fire

    Red shots destroy chasers. Pull a chaser into the line of fire and watch it pop. Bars wipe out squares, so grab them first.

  • Your thumb never hides the dot

    Drag anywhere and the dot follows your finger's motion at the speed you choose. Prefer the original? Switch to Direct.

A pure game.

No pop-ups between runs, nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for. One free Second Chance every run, and the same danger on every screen.
