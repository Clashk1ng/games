How can we help?

How to play Avoid Me, tips, answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

How to play

  1. Drag to move

    Drag anywhere on the screen. The white dot moves with your finger, so your thumb never hides it.

  2. Collect the squares

    Every white square you touch is one point.

  3. Avoid everything red

    Chasers, sweeping bars, aimed launchers and ring launchers. Each one warns you before it arrives.

Tips

  • Red shots destroy chasers. Lure them into the fire!
  • Bars sweep the screen and wipe out squares. Head for the gap early.
  • A red strip pulsing on an edge means a bar is coming; the dark notch shows where the gap will be.
  • Launchers aim where you are when they fire. Keep moving.
  • Ring shots fly straight out. Slip between them.

Controls and settings

Pause
The pause button at the top right. The game also pauses when you leave the app and counts down 3-2-1 when you come back.
Settings
SETTINGS on the home screen: Music, Sound effects, Vibration (on devices that support it), Reduce motion, Controls and Drag speed.
Controls
Drag (default): the dot moves with your finger's motion, at 0.8x to 1.6x. Direct: the dot runs to where your finger is.
Second Chance
After a game over, SECOND CHANCE continues the run once: nearby hazards burst away and play resumes after 3-2-1.
After a run
Tap RETRY or anywhere on the screen to go again, or MENU to go home.

Questions

What is the difference between Drag and Direct?

With Drag, the dot copies your finger's motion (faster or slower with Drag speed), so you can steer from anywhere on the screen. With Direct, the dot runs to the spot under your finger, like the original game.

What is Second Chance?

Once per run, after a game over, you can continue: nearby hazards burst away and you get a 3-2-1 before play resumes. It is free.

What got me?

The game-over card names what caught you (a chaser, a bar, an aimed shot or a ring) and gives a tip for next time.

Is it really free?

Yes. Avoid Me is free to download, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Everything in the game is available from the start.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Avoid Me works fully offline, so it plays fine on a plane or with mobile data switched off.

Where is my progress saved? Can I lose it?

Your best score and settings stay on your device. It is saved on your device the moment it changes. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move to a new device unless a backup or a phone-to-phone transfer brings the app data along.

Can I play on iPad?

Yes. Avoid Me is made for iPhone and iPad. Spawn rates adapt to the screen size, so an iPad run is exactly as dangerous as an iPhone run.

What does Reduce motion do?

It tones down moving effects such as screen shake and particle bursts. It follows your device's Reduce Motion setting until you change it in the game's Settings.

Does the game collect any data?

No. Nothing about you or your play ever leaves your device. The privacy policy has the details.
