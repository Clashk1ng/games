How can we help?

How to play Leapline, tips, answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

How to play

  1. Watch the line

    Your cube follows the dotted track by itself and picks up the coins on it.

  2. Tap to hop

    Tap anywhere to hop over a single yellow tile. Landing on a tile ends the run.

  3. Hold to fly

    Keep your finger down to float over a row of tiles. Let go to come down sooner.

Tips

  • Single tiles need a tap; rows of three need a hold.
  • A tap made a split second before landing still counts: the cube jumps the moment it lands. Landing on a tile still ends the run.
  • Portals send you to a different exit, so watch where you come out.
  • Each loop of the track is faster than the last. A banner shows the new loop and speed.
  • Two thumbs work too: the hold belongs to the finger that started the jump.

Controls and settings

Pause
The pause button at the top right. The game also pauses when you leave the app and counts down 3-2-1 when you come back.
Settings
The gear button on the home screen: Sound effects, Vibration (on devices that support it) and Reduce motion.
Cubes
The cube button on the home screen. Unlock cubes with coins and choose the one you ride.
After a run
Tap RETRY or anywhere on the screen to go again, or MENU to go home.

Questions

Why did I crash when I tapped?

A tap hops over one tile. A row of tiles needs a hold. Landing on a tile always ends the run, even if you pressed again just before.

How do I unlock new cubes?

Collect coins during runs, then open the cube screen from the home screen. Each cube shows its price; the game-over card shows how close you are to the next one.

Is there music?

Leapline has sound effects only. Turn them off in Settings.

Is it really free?

Yes. Leapline is free to download, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Cubes are unlocked only with coins collected while playing.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Leapline works fully offline, so it plays fine on a plane or with mobile data switched off.

Where is my progress saved? Can I lose it?

Your best score, coins and cubes stay on your device. It is saved on your device the moment it changes. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move to a new device unless a backup or a phone-to-phone transfer brings the app data along.

Can I play on iPad?

Yes. Leapline is made for iPhone and iPad. Phones and iPads show the same stretch of track ahead, so neither gets an easier view.

What does Reduce motion do?

It tones down moving effects such as screen shake and particle bursts. It follows your device's Reduce Motion setting until you change it in the game's Settings.

Does the game collect any data?

No. Nothing about you or your play ever leaves your device. The privacy policy has the details.
