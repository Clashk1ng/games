How it plays

One line. Two moves.

The cube runs the track by itself. Your job is timing, and the track gets faster every loop.

  • Tap to hop, hold to fly

    A tap clears a single yellow tile. Hold to float over a whole row. Press a split second early and the cube still jumps the moment it lands.

  • A hand-built track with portals

    7 hand-built sections of towers, crystals and coin trails. Portals warp you to a different exit, and every loop runs faster.

  • Coins unlock cubes

    Collect coins along the line and spend them on 6 more cubes to ride. Every one is earned by playing.

A pure game.

No pop-ups between runs, nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for. Every tile on every route can be cleared at every speed, so when you miss, you know why.
